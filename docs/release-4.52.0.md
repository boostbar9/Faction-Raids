# 4.52.0 — Building and camp reliability

The complete Building and camp reliability package uses the CurseForge **Release** channel (`releaseType: release`). Release-channel selection does not establish universal modpack compatibility. Exact source, artifact and publication records are listed below.

## What changes

- One Building section brings automatic perimeter review, all six existing placeable plans and nearby construction progress together. Dark panels, native block-model thumbnails, readable costs and compact layouts retain Minecraft fonts and ordinary controls.
- Automatic walls follow the complete claimed boundary, leaving shared internal edges open. New projects cost one flat **64 faction Treasury emeralds**, with blocks supplied separately and no size or section surcharge. Manual plan prices and claim purchase prices are unchanged.
- New perimeter, Wall Section and Wall Corner bodies are hollow underneath the oak walkway. Visible outer faces and parapets are retained; manual modules retain end caps. Terrain footings remain intact. In the flat 5×5 cobblestone/oak example, the recipe falls from 6,600 to 3,900 blocks; this 40.9% reduction is specific to that geometry.
- Larger work is partitioned into bounded native Workers sections under one durable project, payment and reservation. Progress reflects verified world placement; unavailable sections or uncertain recovery pause rather than disappear or count as complete.
- Native shovel rendering, picking, blueprint transforms, builder goals, supplies and schedules stay in use. Guarded clearing handles only unchanged supported single-cell plants. Player edits, solids, inventories, unsafe neighbors and lost permissions stop work. Cavities are protected clearance, never AIR excavation jobs.
- A pinned native multi-target approach mismatch now uses the actual verified reached endpoint, preventing the reproduced stall on a previously completed wall.
- Interrupted native inventory callbacks retain durable cleanup obligations and cannot be replayed automatically. Authenticated hand-only provenance now survives completion/cancellation, allowing equal load-time main-hand/inventory mirrors to be rebound without changing any item amount, NBT or capability. It grants no work or storage authority. Authenticated whole-project cancellation preserves placed blocks and does not refund the commission or consumed materials.
- Camp placement gains clearer bounded failure reasons, exact registered guard lookup and a narrowly matched Recruits 1.15.2 scout-initialization correction. Safe natural forest clearing, leveling and shallow-water fallback retain claim/build protection. Some locations genuinely cannot support a camp.

## Updating a world

Back up the world first and install the same Siege Overhaul build on the server and every client. Network protocol 19 requires matching peers.

Already-paid/accepted jobs retain their exact saved geometry, native blueprint, material state and payment, including older solid walls and previously paid legacy perimeter commissions. They are not hollowed, repriced, recharged or refunded. Changed unpaid previews need fresh review. Existing world blocks are not carved out.

Protected construction and post-load inventory verification for previously protected builders require the audited **Workers 2.0.3 and Recruits 1.15.2** runtime. Other companion versions pause those jobs and reload checks; ordinary unmarked legacy native workers retain their existing behavior. Minecraft 1.20.1, Forge 47.x and Java 17 remain required, as do Small Ships and Siege Weapons. Runtime evidence uses Forge 47.4.16, Small Ships 2.0.0-b1.4 and Siege Weapons 0.2.5. Optional integrations remain optional.

A complete initial perimeter review requires loaded terrain and passes finite permission, storage, geometry, scan and serialization bounds. Oversized or unsafe plans are rejected as a whole. Later unloaded sections wait; no force-loading or silent partial acceptance is introduced. Default Recruits chunk loading may keep the worker's vicinity loaded.

The receipt keeps the original commissioning owner as history; later native ownership transfer does not invalidate hand-only verification or grant construction/storage permission. The hand-only history retains at most 4,096 builder identities per Overworld ledger; existing matching builders remain reusable at capacity. It is not a job reservation or permission grant. Authenticated old protected jobs migrate while their original proof is available. Missing or inconsistent proof can defer cleanup; an unavailable/dead old builder may require recovery review rather than simply loading it. Post-load verification with active item use pauses without stopping use or guessing at inventory.
