# Hero casting and Olympian weapons

The three area casters (Tempest, Inferno and Undertow) charge for 20 ticks before releasing their existing ability. The server owns the cast, rechecks the target and every victim at release, and retains the old cooldown across reloads. Interrupted casts do not refund the cooldown. No terrain damage is added.

Tracking clients receive entity ID plus UUID, role, phase, game time and release origin. Late trackers receive an active wind-up snapshot. Visual caches clear on world changes, expire after their phase and cap at 64 casts. Local particles share a 128-per-tick budget. The Hero visuals settings control poses, particles, distance, volume and reduced flashes.

The model adapter shares the native Recruits humanoid parts and delegates native rendering, so armor layers and held items follow the casting arms without replacing companion geometry. Source reviewed: Talhanation Recruits 1.15.2 commit cff03e085d65653406a8b6ddcdd0ebff615c3e48 (RecruitHumanRenderer, RecruitVillagerRenderer, RecruitVillagerModel). This source review is not a live companion-mod playtest.

Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks 1.20.1-legacy commit b928995887aa362467fa999a9c9fb86419f00f2d was an architectural reference for synchronized cast phases and client geometry. No code, textures, models, animation files or sounds from that project are included. This implementation adds no mandatory dependency.

Weapon appearance uses existing SiegeOlympianPatron loot tags or the new SiegeHeroWeaponRole identity tag. Hero equipment receives its tag when equipped; existing heroes' matching named main-hand equipment is tagged on their next normal hero update. Other items, including items with explicit CustomModelData, retain their original model override. Item IDs, enchantments, damage and names do not change. Untagged items in old player inventories are not guessed from their names.

Original cuboid geometry is generated by tools/generate_olympian_weapon_models.py using Minecraft block texture references. All 20 hero main-hand weapons and tagged Olympian swords, bows, crossbows, pickaxes, hoes and fishing rods are supported. Tridents, shields and armor retain their native renderers; replacing those would also need projectile/blocking and companion-renderer coverage. This first pass does not animate every passive hero trait.

Automated checks cover wind-up/release/cancellation, hostility and cooldowns, packet validation, shared model parts/armor copies, skin selection, saved-item preservation, draw states and resource geometry. Interactive appearance, shader packs and third-party renderer replacements remain untested.

Crossbow skins distinguish empty, drawing, loaded-arrow and loaded-firework states. Model selection reads the existing charged-projectile NBT without changing it. String endpoint regressions check all draw states against the fixed limb tips.

The creative catalog reuses the production loot factories and hero equipment factory. It normalizes tab stacks to one item and deduplicates equal item/NBT entries; rebuilding the tab never shares mutable stacks. The Olympian Arts book documents all twenty hero traits. Tempest, Inferno and Undertow books are Creative-only casting tools, not new survival drops. Their existing hero effects, wind-up and saved cooldowns are enforced by the server; player targets, allies and unmarked mobs are excluded. Early release cancels the cast. Native Recruits eggs remain unowned until hired through the companion's normal UI; outfitting happens before insertion and failed spawns keep the egg.

Draw stages match the Minecraft 1.20.1 bow model predicates (0.65 and 0.9 of 20 ticks) and crossbow predicates (0.58 and 1.0 of the enchantment-adjusted charge duration). Previously dividing the duration into thirds advanced both skins too early. Release returns to the idle model; charged arrow/firework selection still takes precedence. Source assets: https://github.com/misode/mcmeta/blob/1.20.1-assets/assets/minecraft/models/item/bow.json and https://github.com/misode/mcmeta/blob/1.20.1-assets/assets/minecraft/models/item/crossbow.json (mirrors of Minecraft's versioned assets). Native hand transforms are checked for both arms after a casting pose. This is automated coverage, not an in-game visual confirmation.
