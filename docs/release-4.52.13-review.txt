Production assessment
This release changes player feedback and documentation only; placement rules, prices, claim requirements and dependency metadata are unchanged. DefenseStructures.claimDenial replaces the single sentence "The whole structure must be inside your core's claim and allow building" with a reason that names the chunk a manual plan reaches (chunk coordinates and block ranges) and states whether that chunk is not part of the claim holding the Siege Core, belongs to another faction, or is a different claim of the player's own faction. DefenseStructures.protectedDenial separately reports server spawn protection or the world border for the exact block. A failed claim provider (FTB Chunks, Open Parties and Claims, or a Villager Recruits bridge that disabled itself after a lookup threw) is reported as unknown ownership through the PROVIDER_FAILURE text, checked before the dependency gate, after core-claim resolution and after every ownership lookup, instead of being presented as a rival claim, unclaimed land, lost territory or a missing dependency. PerimeterConstruction.territoryDenial applies the same structure to automatic perimeters, which continue to use the complete same-faction territory. Per-chunk permission results, including permitted chunks, are cached once per chunk with Optional so a valid plan performs one claim lookup per chunk. ClaimBridge.providerBroken() and RecruitsClaimsBridge.failed() expose the failure state, and ClaimBridge.diagnosticStatus() reports "Recruits: ERROR (placement blocked)" in that case. /siegeoverhaul help gains one line stating that factions and claims are managed in Villager Recruits through its faction screen (U by default) and claim map (M by default) and that there is no /faction command; README and CHANGELOG mirror this. Problem strings remain within the 256-character preview bound and are wrapped by the plan review HUD.

Codex (151be7f): the same caching gap (P1); a broken FTB Chunks or Open Parties provider was reported as a foreign claim (P2); and the perimeter scope wording (P2). Fixed in 6ea5a4a with Optional caches, a provider-failure pre-check and PROVIDER_FAILURE text, and corrected documentation. Codex (6ea5a4a): the provider could fail during the lookup itself after the pre-check passed (P2); and a chunk in a different claim of the same faction was told to claim land it already owned (P3). Fixed in bbec2e0 by rechecking providerBroken() after each isForeignClaim call and by recognizing same-faction sibling claims through RecruitsClaimsBridge.isChunkOwnedBy. Codex (bbec2e0): a Recruits lookup that threw mid-session disabled that bridge without being counted as a broken provider, so unverified land was called unclaimed or lost territory (P2). Fixed in 750700e with RecruitsClaimsBridge.failed(), its inclusion in providerBroken(), and rechecks after the Recruits ownership lookups. Codex (750700e): the new check was unreachable when the failure happened before prepare's availability gate or inside resolveDefendingClaim (P2); and /siegeoverhaul compat showed Recruits as off rather than failed (P3). Fixed in 4d099e4 by checking providerBroken() before the dependency gate and after claim resolution in both prepare methods and by reporting the Recruits error state in diagnosticStatus(). Each fix carries a named regression in DefenseStructuresTest (26 cases), PerimeterConstructionTest (15 cases) or ClaimBridgeFailureTest (10 cases).
